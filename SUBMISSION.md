*This is a submission for the [Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 3: Slow and Steady](https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week3-2026-10-19).*

The first version of this was one calmer sentence. That is not the project. Mumblebox takes the message you will not send and writes three different replies: soft, brief, and plain. You pick one. The page does not send it.

Open the local desk from the project folder. The mark is three cards and a seal. The photograph is the same desk: one crumpled note, three written cards.

The samples are made up. Same rule as [CloseTab](https://dev.to/fabc14/closetab-counts-a-stalls-night-note-without-inventing-a-rupee-3ofa) and [Daniel Nwaneri's day-book](https://dev.to/dannwaneri/bank-alerts-to-a-day-book-with-a-local-gemma-21dl): Gemma may choose the words, and the code rejects an invented number. Three identical replies are rejected too.

## What you get

Paste who it is for, and the blunt note. Press Draft three.

| Tone | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Soft | The one you can send without starting a fight. |
| Brief | Two sentences. The request, then stop. |
| Plain | No apology. The fact and the ask. |

A command-line nap does the same thing to the first file in `inbox/` and leaves the rest. Undo puts that note back and deletes the three drafts.

## Why it stays on this machine

The note is the version you would not paste into someone else's account. Gemma 2 2B runs through Ollama here. There is no per-note bill. When the model invents a number, or returns the same sentence three times, that answer is thrown out.
